# Capability — what you can do

This layer is the twin's working knowledge: your skills, the domains you know cold, the kinds of problems you can solve, and — importantly — *how* you go about solving them.

The goal isn't a résumé. It's enough that a model could reason the way you reason.

## Prompts

Copy this into a new file (e.g. `skills.md`) and answer what you like:

- **Domains.** What subjects or fields do you actually know well? Rank them if you can.
- **What you build / make / do.** Concrete outputs — what comes out of your hands or your head?
- **Judgment.** In your area, what's a call you'd make confidently that a newcomer would get wrong?
- **Method.** When you approach a hard problem, what are your first moves? Your habits? Your shortcuts?
- **Tools.** What do you reach for — software, techniques, references?
- **What you're learning now.** Where's your edge — the thing you're actively getting better at?

## What this becomes

These files are the twin's *capability layer* — what it can do on your behalf. Paired with the values layer, it's not just "what would she do" but "what would she do *and should she*."
