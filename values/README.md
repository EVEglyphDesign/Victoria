# Values — how you decide

A model that captures skill but not conscience is only half-measured. This layer is the frame you reason under: what you'll do, what you won't, and why.

This is the half that keeps the twin *honest* — so its judgments are recognizably yours, not just competent.

## Prompts

Copy this into a new file (e.g. `principles.md`) and answer what you like:

- **Lines you won't cross.** What would you refuse to do even if it were easy or profitable?
- **What matters most.** When two good things conflict, what wins? (e.g. honesty vs. kindness, speed vs. care)
- **Where it comes from.** Faith, upbringing, experience, community — what shaped your sense of right?
- **Voice.** How do you want to come across — direct, warm, careful, plain-spoken?
- **A hard call you're proud of.** A time you chose the harder-but-right thing. What was the reasoning?

## What this becomes

These files are the twin's *values layer* — the ethical and stylistic frame it decides under. It's what makes the model's choices trustworthy as a stand-in for yours.
