# Data export — optional, and always your own

If and when you want to add real data to the twin, the right way is to export it **from accounts you control**, on your own terms. Nothing here is required, and nothing is collected for you. You bring what you choose.

This folder is where you'd drop those exports (or notes about them). Below are the official self-service export paths for common accounts. These are the "download *my* data" tools each service provides to its own account holders — you run them, you get a file, you decide what to keep.

## Self-export tools

- **Google (Gmail, Drive, Photos, YouTube, etc.)** — [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com)
- **Instagram** — Settings → Accounts Center → *Your information and permissions* → **Download your information**
- **Apple** — [Apple Data & Privacy](https://privacy.apple.com) → *Get a copy of your data*
- **Microsoft / OneDrive** — [Microsoft privacy dashboard](https://account.microsoft.com/privacy)
- **LinkedIn** — Settings → Data Privacy → **Get a copy of your data**
- **Notion, Dropbox, most SaaS** — each has an *Export* under account/workspace settings

## How to use what you export

1. Export from a service you own.
2. Skim it — keep only what actually says something true about you (identity, capability, or values).
3. Summarize it into a short markdown note in the matching folder. Raw dumps aren't as useful as a paragraph in your own words.
4. Delete anything you don't want in the model. Restraint is a feature.

## Ground rule

Only your own accounts, only data you're comfortable modeling. If you'd hesitate to say it out loud about yourself, it probably doesn't belong in the twin.

*(A `.gitignore` in this repo keeps common export formats out of version control by default, so you don't accidentally commit a raw dump.)*
