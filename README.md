# Victoria

An onboarding starter kit for building a **digital twin of yourself** — a working model of your knowledge, judgment, and voice that can help with R&D.

This repository is a *frame*. It comes with structure, prompts, and one shared artifact to look at — but the contents are yours to fill. Nothing in here is scraped or pre-collected about you. You decide what goes in, one file at a time.

> **New here? Start with [`DAY-1.md`](DAY-1.md)** — a 30-minute on-ramp.
>
> Starting point: Monday. No rush to finish anything today. Read this page, open the map in `artifacts/`, and fill in whatever's easy first.

---

## The idea in one picture

A digital twin is not a copy of your files. It's a model measured from several independent directions until it locks into place — the same way a point on a map is fixed by taking bearings from three landmarks. If enough independent lines cross at the same spot, that spot isn't a guess anymore. It's confirmed.

We use **three vectors**, and each one becomes a **layer** of your twin:

| Vector | The question it answers | Twin layer | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | Where do you come from — what makes the model *you* and no one else? | Identity substrate | [`identity/`](identity/) |
| **Capability** | What can you do — what do you know how to build, decide, judge? | Capability layer | [`capability/`](capability/) |
| **Values** | Under what frame do you decide what's worth doing? | Values layer | [`values/`](values/) |

Fill the three folders and the point in the middle — *you* — comes into focus. That's the triangulation.

## How to work through it

1. **Read the three folder READMEs.** Each one explains its layer and gives you plain-language prompts.
2. **Fill what's easy first.** A few sentences beats a blank file. You can always come back.
3. **Bring your own data on your own terms.** [`data-export/`](data-export/) shows how to export data from accounts *you* control, if and when you want to add it. Nothing is required.
4. **Commit as you go.** Each file you add is one more bearing. The twin sharpens with every one.

---

## Ground rules

- **Consent-first.** Everything here is contributed by you, about you. No third-party data gathering.
- **You own it.** This repo is yours. You choose what stays private, what's shared, and what's deleted.
- **Nothing is mandatory.** Blank is fine. Partial is fine. This is a frame, not a form.
