# Identity — the substrate

This is the layer you're *made of*, not measured against. Where you come from, the story that's yours, the base facts that make the model you and nobody else.

There are no wrong answers here. A few honest sentences per prompt is plenty. Skip anything that doesn't fit.

## Prompts

Copy this into a new file (e.g. `about-me.md`) and answer what you like:

- **Origin.** Where are you from — place, family, lineage? What roots matter to you?
- **Language.** What languages live in your head? Which one do you think in?
- **The through-line.** If someone traced your life back, what's the thread that runs through all of it?
- **What's fixed.** What about you doesn't change regardless of context — the things that are just *true* of you?
- **How you'd introduce yourself** to someone who needs to understand you quickly.

## What this becomes

These files are the twin's *identity substrate* — the ground truth the rest of the model is anchored to. Capability and values describe what you do and how you choose; identity is who is doing the choosing.
